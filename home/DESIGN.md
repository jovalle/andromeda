# Andromeda Home

Native utility surface. Preserve the current wallpaper and Android app icons.
Four evenly spaced columns, configured rows at the top, four dock items at the
bottom. A small All apps control opens the full catalog and system settings.
Use Android's native sans serif, 52dp icons, two-line 13sp labels, at least 48dp
touch targets, and generous row spacing. White labels have a dark shadow and a
dark wallpaper scrim for contrast. No clock, search widget, decorative panels,
or animation beyond platform press and activity transitions.
